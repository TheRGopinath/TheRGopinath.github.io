M.Sc. thesis · 2026–present · “Bombay Magic”

Clifford-augmented matrix product states for quantum reservoir computing

with Dr. Sai Vinjanampathy, IIT Bombay

Matrix product states (MPS) are good at representing low-entanglement states, and Clifford circuits can be simulated efficiently on a classical computer. Clifford-augmented MPS (CAMPS) combine the two: entanglement that a Clifford circuit can account for is moved out of the MPS, which keeps the tensor network small. I'm building a quantum reservoir computer on top of this representation: designing the reservoir, the input encoding and the readout observables, and benchmarking it.

  • CAMPS
  • Tensor networks
  • Stabilizer formalism
  • Quantum reservoir computing
Summer 2026 · “Indiana Fever”

Sparse Pauli propagation meets transfer tensors

with Prof. Nicholas Mayhall, Indiana University

A framework that combines sparse Pauli propagation with the transfer tensor method. Short-time Heisenberg-picture trajectories are used to build subsystem dynamical maps, which then carry observables out to long times. I implemented it in Julia, including Pauli-subspace projection, transfer-tensor construction, and memory and stability diagnostics.

On spin-boson models it agrees with direct Pauli propagation and exact small-system references in the low-coupling regime. I also analysed the errors from Trotterization, sparse truncation and finite memory windows. A manuscript is in preparation.

  • Non-Markovian dynamics
  • Pauli propagation
  • Julia
Semester project · 2026

Eigenstate thermalization and quantum chaos in spin chains

with Dr. Soumya Bera, IIT Bombay

Exact diagonalization of the mixed-field Ising chain in Python, resolving its spin-flip and reflection symmetries. I tracked the crossover from Poisson to GOE level statistics as the longitudinal field is turned on.

I then tested the eigenstate thermalization hypothesis (ETH) through local observables, half-chain entanglement entropy, and how eigenstate fluctuations shrink with system size.

  • ETH
  • Random-matrix statistics
  • Exact diagonalization
  • Python

Strange attractors

with Dr. Chandrakala Meena

Bifurcation and sensitivity analysis of twelve strange-attractor systems in Python, characterising periodic, quasiperiodic and chaotic regimes, and comparing how well Euler, RK2 and RK4 resolve the transitions.
